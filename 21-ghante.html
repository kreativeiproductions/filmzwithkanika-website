The story

What if 21 hours made all the difference?

Four friends plan a harmless staged kidnapping. What begins as a prank spirals into a dark, unexpected reality — where one wrong decision changes everything. As the hours run out, trust breaks, fear takes control, and every twist asks what friendship and time are really worth.

Behind the scenes

The script took a month of twelve-hour days. The shoot took four — in a forest, with a large cast, no budget for lights, and a core crew of four or five working sixteen hours straight, 7am to midnight. When night fell, some scenes were lit with nothing but mobile phones.

Two more months went into the edit, and I composed the background score myself. Within a month of its release on YouTube, the film's response grew FilmzwithKanika enough to be monetised — and later it won Best Short Film at the Nawada International Film Festival.
