Meet the filmmaker

Kanika Bansal

Writer · Director · Producer · Founder, FilmzwithKanika

More than twenty-three years in production — starting in 2003 with corporate video editing, then weddings from 2012 — and then, in 2019, a phone call: “We need a short film. Will you do it?” I said yes without a second thought. Ten films later, I have never been to film school. Everything I know, I learned on set, in the edit, and by reading scripts line by line against the films they became.

“Films aren't made with money. They're made with people and intention.” — on shooting Ek Mulaqaat for ₹10,000
